I think it perfect
To receive a Valentine from you
During Augusts’ warm nights and thunder heads—
A month of loose clothes and sweat.

After all, they are the dog days, when drought
Prevails, plants retreat inward, and the sun is torrid.
I think love lounges in the shade
Of an eighty-foot Elm in my backyard,
Too lazy to do anything right now.

Without another tree close enough
To hang a hammock, I keep hearing
Wright’s last line, I have wasted my life.
Instead, in the heat of the day, because I slept
Too late once again, went to bed too late
Once again, I shovel rocks and dig and thirst.
