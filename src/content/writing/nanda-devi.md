She dies high
above the tree line
amongst glaciated seracs
and wind shorn gendarmes—

Asphyxiated blue,
voluptuously lost
lips. chapped, cracked,
high cheek bones.

The tent in the wind
half-mast carrying
cries across
hushed blues.

Seven thousand feet below
spring is verdant
and the mountain
a backdrop for wildflowers.
