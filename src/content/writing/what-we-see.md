the moon is reflection, what we feel is body

With each turn of earth, moon chases:
The reflected eye of sun into dark.
Look further, under stilled water,
Deeper, to porous rock dried hollow,
When rivers ran red, cracked heart of earth.

Moon messaged storms from open oceans,
Over valleys, over hills, into mountains,
Trickling, tears turned algae with cattail lashes.
Can this water be pulled by moon?
Can this water feel want of distant love?
