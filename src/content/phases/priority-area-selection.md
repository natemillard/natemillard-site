Need alone should not drive the choice. Existing relationships, trust and
momentum count equally.

This phase is short on new skills and long on consequences. It is mostly a
decision made with what Phase 1 produced. A chapter that guessed its way
through Phase 1 will guess here too, and will not be able to defend the answer.
