Explicitly not a checklist, not a partner quota, and not a rush to sign
agreements.

This is also where cross-sector relationships actually begin, with
organizations you meet in the neighborhood rather than ones you already had on
a list.

Five of the six load-bearing skills here are also load-bearing in Phase 1. The
sixth, Building Cross-Sector Partnerships, is the one thing this phase asks for
that Phase 1 cannot rehearse for you.
