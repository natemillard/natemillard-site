A one-year divisional assignment, and mostly a coaching role rather than a
delivery one. The distinction that organises the work is between an
organisation that serves a community and one that builds capacity with it —
which sounds like a matter of emphasis and is actually a different job.

Needs-based delivery produces a plan only the organisation can execute.
Asset-based work starts from what a place already has: the trusted people who
hold no title, the groups with no letterhead and real reach. It is slower and
it survives your departure.

Most of what I do is help teams tell the difference between an organisational
output and a community-level outcome, and then hand ownership over — which is
harder than it sounds, because handing over looks like doing less.
